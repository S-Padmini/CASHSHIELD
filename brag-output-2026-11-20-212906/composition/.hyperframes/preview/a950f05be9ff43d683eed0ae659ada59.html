The Problem

The Challenge

Indian MSMEs often face cash-flow shortages when upcoming supplier payments exceed the cash currently available, even though customers still owe them money.

They need an intelligent way to decide which payments to prioritize or delay, when to accelerate receivables, and whether financing is necessary while protecting essential operating cash.

How CashShield Addresses It

CashShield recommends a feasible, lowest-estimated-cost combination of financial actions while respecting minimum cash reserves and mandatory payment obligations.
